HOLY SCRIPTURE

Open the Book

The Bible is a library of books written across many centuries. Catholics read Scripture within the life and teaching of the Church.

✠

“The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.”

Psalm 23:1

Good places to begin

01

Gospels

Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John tell the story of Jesus. Mark is short; John has a distinctive theological style.

02

Psalms

Prayers and songs expressing praise, grief, thanksgiving, trust, and longing for God.

03

Acts

The story of the early Church and the spread of the Gospel.

04

Genesis

Creation, humanity’s calling, the fall, and the stories of the patriarchs.

A simple Bible method

  1. Read: What does the passage say?
  2. Notice: What stands out?
  3. Reflect: What might this reveal about God?
  4. Pray: Respond in your own words.
  5. Return: Write one sentence in your journal.
