A LONG STORY

A tiny Catholic timeline

The Catholic Church’s history stretches across two millennia. This is only a starting point.

c. 30s

The first Christians

After Jesus’ death and Resurrection, his disciples proclaimed the Gospel and formed communities.

1st century

The apostles & early Church

Christian communities spread throughout the Roman world; the New Testament writings emerged within this early Christian context.

325

Council of Nicaea

The bishops gathered at Nicaea and produced the first form of what became the Nicene Creed.

1054

East–West Schism

Longstanding differences between Eastern and Western Christianity culminated in a rupture traditionally dated to 1054.

1517+

Reformation era

Western Christianity experienced major divisions; the Catholic Church responded with reform and the Council of Trent.

1962–1965

Second Vatican Council

The council addressed liturgy, Scripture, ecumenism, the laity, and the Church’s mission in the modern world.

Today

A living Church

Catholic communities continue to worship, teach, serve, evangelize, and wrestle with how to live the Gospel.
