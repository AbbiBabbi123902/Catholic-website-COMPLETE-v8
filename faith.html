THE CATHOLIC FAITH

What Catholics believe

At the center of Catholic Christianity is Jesus Christ: his life, death, Resurrection, and the invitation to know and love God.

The Creed

The Nicene Creed summarizes core Christian beliefs about the Father, Son, Holy Spirit, the Church, Baptism, resurrection, and eternal life. Catholics normally profess it at Sunday Mass.

The four marks of the Church

Ⅰ

One

The Church is one because her source is the one God and her founder is Christ.

Ⅱ

Holy

The Church is holy because Christ is holy and the Church is called to holiness.

Ⅲ

Catholic

“Catholic” means universal: the Church is sent to all peoples and places.

Ⅳ

Apostolic

The Church is built on the apostles and continues their mission through apostolic succession.

The seven sacraments

BaptismBirth into Christian life.
ConfirmationStrengthening and sealing by the Holy Spirit.
EucharistParticipation in Christ's Body and Blood.
ReconciliationForgiveness of sins and restored communion with God.
Anointing of the SickGrace and strength for the seriously ill.
Holy OrdersOrdination to the diaconate, priesthood, or episcopate.
MatrimonyThe sacramental covenant of marriage.
