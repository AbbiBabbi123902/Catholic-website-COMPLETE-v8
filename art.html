SACRED BEAUTY

Symbols everywhere

Catholic churches are full of visual language. Objects, gestures, colors, architecture, and art can teach before a single word is spoken.

✝

The Cross

Recalls Christ’s Passion and death and points Christians toward the Resurrection.

🕯

Candles

Light can symbolize Christ as the Light of the World and the presence of prayer.

📿

Rosary

Beads used to keep track of prayers while meditating on mysteries of Christ’s life.

🪽

Angels

Spiritual creatures who serve God and can appear in Scripture as messengers.

🕊

Dove

Can symbolize the Holy Spirit, especially in depictions of Baptism or Pentecost.

☀

Monstrance

A vessel used to display the consecrated Eucharistic host, especially during adoration.

👑

Mary’s Crown

Mary may be depicted crowned as Queen in Catholic devotion.

🌹

Flowers

Can express beauty, honor, sacrifice, life, and hope of resurrection depending on context.
