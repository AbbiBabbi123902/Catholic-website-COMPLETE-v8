THE SAINTS

Friends on the way

Catholics honor the saints because their lives point toward Christ. Saints are not worshiped; Catholics ask for their prayers and seek to imitate their holiness.

✿

Assisi

St. Francis

Known for simplicity, poverty, care for creation, and love for Christ. Feast: October 4.

✦

Calcutta

St. Teresa of Calcutta

Known for serving people who were poor and sick. Feast: September 5.

☩

Siena

St. Catherine of Siena

Dominican tertiary, mystic, theologian, and Doctor of the Church. Feast: April 29.

🕊

Lisieux

St. Thérèse

The “Little Flower,” known for her “little way” of trust and ordinary love. Feast: October 1.

📚

Hippo

St. Augustine

Bishop and influential theologian; author of Confessions and The City of God. Feast: August 28.

🌹

Mexico

St. Juan Diego

Associated with the apparitions of Our Lady of Guadalupe in 1531. Feast: December 9.
